Eligibility

Who can apply

Any school, any field

Undergraduates at any college or university can apply, in any field — from biology to history to studio art.

Any stage of the work

Projects don't need to be finished. Preliminary data, a semester project, or work still in progress all count as research.

No experience required

Most people presenting at URC will be first-time presenters. No prior conference experience is expected.

Deadlines

Two deadlines

Space is limited. Priority applicants get first pick of the schedule.

Priority deadline September 10
  • Guaranteed review
  • Decision by September 14
  • Guaranteed spot on the schedule
Final deadline September 25
  • Reviewed only if space remains
  • No guaranteed spot
  • Decisions on a rolling basis

Applying to present also reserves your seat — no need to RSVP separately if you've applied.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Can I apply if I'm not a UNC student?
Yes. URC is open to undergraduates at any college or university, not just UNC.
Is it free?
Yes — free to attend and free to present.
What size can my poster be, and what does printing cost?

What if I've never presented before?
Most presenters at URC haven't either. The pre-conference workshops cover what you need.
What if my research isn't finished?
Work in progress is fine. Preliminary results and early-stage data count as research here.
Do I need my faculty mentor's permission?
No formal permission is required to apply. It's worth telling your mentor or PI you're presenting.
What if I can't afford to print a poster?
Tell us when you apply. We've set aside funds for printing and can point you to campus resources.
